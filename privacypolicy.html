Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Accuracy Electric LLC, we value your privacy. This policy explains how we handle the information you submit through our website contact form.

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out our free estimate form, we voluntarily collect the following information to help evaluate your project:

  • Phone Number
  • Email Address
  • Job Location & Project Details

2. How We Use Your Information

We only use the details you provide to contact you back, discuss your electrical needs, and provide an accurate job estimate.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to ensure your contact details remain safe.
